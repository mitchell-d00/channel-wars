# OpenAI TV — The Broadcast Empire

**OpenAI TV** is a free, original, browser-based TV station management game inspired by classic TV tycoon games such as *Mad TV*. Build a broadcasting empire from a tiny local channel, right from your iPad or desktop browser.

**Play offline:** open `index.html`. Everything is included in that **single HTML file** — styling, graphics, game logic, and save handling. No build tools, sign-ups, external packages, or connection required.

## Gameplay

- **Broadcasting tower:** Eight rooms for management, programming, the film market, advertising, news, engineering, competition, and finances.
- **Programming:** Buy and reuse licenses from a 32-show fictional content catalogue. Match genres, broadcast windows, lengths, and viewer interests.
- **Seven-hour schedule:** Fill every hour from 5 p.m. through 11 p.m.; plan shows and ads separately. Longer programs reserve consecutive hours.
- **Advertising:** Negotiate ad deals, schedule spots, hit audience thresholds before deadlines, and manage penalties.
- **Daily simulation:** Ratings, competing networks, changing viewer demand, promotions, costs, upgrades, debt, and performance reports.
- **Station growth:** Expand audience reach with transmitters, invest in the newsroom and the studio, borrow and repay money, earn achievements.
- **iPad-friendly:** Responsive touch controls and a high-contrast optional dark mode.
- **Save system:** Autosaves in your own browser, with JSON save export/import.

## How to play

1. Visit the Film & TV Market and buy a few programs.
2. At the Advertising Agency, sign contracts you can realistically fulfill.
3. Open the Broadcast Studio and choose programs and advertising for the seven evening slots. Try Auto-fill if you want a suggested starter lineup.
4. Select **1h** to air the next slot or **Go Live** to broadcast the full remaining day.
5. Review your daily report, visit Engineering/Newsroom to improve performance, and repeat.

Your goal is to gain viewers, beat your rivals, and grow a profitable broadcast network. There are no external services or in-app purchases.
